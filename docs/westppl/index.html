              Our philosophy

              Real life first.

              We started with dinners for friends of friends. The rule hasn't changed: everyone at the table should be someone you'd be glad to sit next to.

              • Introductions through people, not feeds.
              • Rooms small enough to learn names.
              • Places that care about the details.
              • Phones down when the food lands.
